02 — THE STORY

Many worlds,
one homeland.

Nepal lies at the meeting point of South and Central Asia. From the snowbound Himalaya in the north to the fertile Terai in the south, its landscape rises from about 60 metres to the summit of Everest in less than 200 kilometres.

For centuries, this dramatic geography has sheltered distinct peoples and cultures. Hindu and Buddhist traditions flourish side by side, shaping the temples, festivals, art, and everyday rhythms of the country.
