04 / CULTURE & HERITAGE

A thousand years
of living beauty.

Nepal is not a place where culture is behind glass. It is a morning bell at a stupa, a family kitchen fragrant with spices, and a masked dancer taking the street at dusk.

Festivals +

Dashain, Tihar, Holi, Buddha Jayanti and Indra Jatra bring neighbourhoods together in colour, light and devotion.

Craft & architecture +

Newari courtyards, carved timber windows, traditional dress, music and dance preserve a rich, continuously evolving heritage.

A table set for sharing +

Discover dal bhat, momo, sel roti and regionally distinctive flavours made for gathering around.

Seasons

Clear skies are common in spring (Mar–May) and autumn (Oct–Nov). Monsoon brings lush landscapes and variable travel.

08 / ABOUT NEPAL

A nation shaped
by altitude & affinity.

Nepal sits between India and China, crossing the Himalayan, hill and Terai regions. Its remarkable biodiversity includes snow leopard habitat in high mountains and rhino grasslands in the south.

From Lumbini, the traditional birthplace of the Buddha, to the art-rich Malla-era Kathmandu Valley, Nepal’s history is carried in its sacred and everyday places. It became a federal democratic republic in 2008.

A country of seven provinces, Nepal is home to diverse ethnic communities and languages. Nepali is the official language, while many mother tongues enrich the country’s shared identity.
